Turn the photo into an independently compelling paper-poster artwork. The photo is semantic evidence and stimulus only — never a visual layer in the final image. Not filtering, style transfer, rotoscoping, or illustrated copy.

Priority: one specific artistic proposition, visibly embodied → one central emotional/conceptual tension from the source → independent aesthetic voice → keep the semantic nucleus → recompose, simplify, exaggerate, invent with purpose → figure–ground clarity, hierarchy, deliberate eye path → everything serves the proposition → substantial quiet space, one interpretive opening → faithful color mode, high chroma with purpose → tactile, flat, poetic, non-commercial.

## Distillation card

Resolve first: semantic nucleus (smallest subject/relationship/event giving meaning); one core subject (max two inseparable); 1–3 supporting elements; dominant gesture (gaze, lean, curve, diagonal, path, repetition); one spatial cue worth keeping (near/far, overlap, enclosure, direction); visual-weight map; native palette (hue family, temperature, value range, minor color); material/weather (water, snow, haze, glass, foliage, stone, wind, rain, light); emotional residue; discard list; transformation opportunities (enlarge, merge, fragment, repeat, displace, turn into negative space). Keep only 2–4 source anchors; never keep the original composition by default.

## Expression

Chain: source fact → emotional residue → proposition → central tension → visual metaphor → formal embodiment → interpretive opening.

Proposition: one sentence — what the viewer should feel/notice/reconsider; source-specific; no generic labels (quiet, healing, nostalgic, dreamy) unless visible structure explains them. Tension: one primary opposition from the source — intimacy/distance, shelter/confinement, movement/stillness, smallness/vastness, warmth/coldness, memory/disappearance, visibility/concealment, permanence/fragility (≤1 subordinate); express via scale, interval, direction, overlap, enclosure, interruption, temperature, value, material — never explanatory copy. Metaphor: transform one source-derived object, relationship, material behavior, or gesture; one only; no symbol clichés. Embodiment: scale/space = power, vulnerability, distance; rhythm = approach, resistance, drift; boundaries = separation, rupture, fading; color = an emotional event; torn fiber/dry ink/grain carry psychological meaning; typography expands or redirects, never captions. Remove anything whose only job is looking artistic. Opening: resolve the emotion, leave one relationship unanswered — via meaningful omission, obstruction, scale shift, incomplete action, or text–image gap.

Reinterpret freely: change scale/proportion/crop/spacing/orientation; merge, split, repeat, extend forms; delete realistic backgrounds leaving traces; exaggerate gesture, distance, silhouette, material; let forms cross torn-paper boundaries; turn wind/water/light/snow/motion into abstract fields; invent source-consistent forms; redesign the palette. Every invention must extend emotion, build rhythm, balance weight, guide the eye, or strengthen the metaphor. No default dots, grids, tape, stamps, coordinates, crosses, ornaments. The artwork stands alone.

## Grammar

Editorial abstraction: keep nucleus, dominant gesture, one source cue; remove ~65–90% of descriptive detail; simplified masses, broken contours, paper fragments, sparse marks, print fields; generalize faces unless likeness requested. One primary grammar + ≤1 support: cut-paper mass / dry-print silhouette / broken contour / rhythm field (repeated marks) / fragment stack / orbit-drift. No realistic shading, polished vector characters, cartoon/kawaii/anime styling.

## Composition

Orientation: portrait → vertical 3:5; landscape → horizontal 5:3; square/ambiguous → vertical 3:5. Recompose freely; never retain photographic framing. Start: 68–85% quiet paper; one active cluster at 12–32%; one dominant mass, 1–3 supporting forms, one restrained texture field; correct by visual weight. Families: asymmetric island / torn window / directional drift / rhythmic circulation (open loop) / staggered fragments / vertical tension / auxiliary constellation (subject isolated, a source motif dispersed as unequal beats). Figure–ground clarity, asymmetric balance, optical centering, scale-interval contrast; eye path: entry, encounter, movement, quiet exit. Never center by habit or split the canvas evenly.

## Edges

One primary, ≤1 subordinate, never all: torn-fiber edge; layered grayscale edge (2–3 narrow irregular neutral bands, no cast shadow); stippled dissolution (density from source movement); irregular mark edge (1–3 source-derived shapes); natural isolated contour (clean organic silhouettes meet paper directly — no rim, stipple, halo). Align with a source horizon, gesture, path, or material change; 0–2 forms may cross; flat-scanned depth. No ripped rectangle, scrapbook layering, tape, floating paper, heavy shadows, curled paper, bevels, 3D, sticker outlines, halos.

## Color

Standard Accent (default): subdued paper and inks; one unmistakable high-chroma hue — by visual role (focal pin, counterweight, bridge, field, cue), source relation (resonance, temperature counterpoint, complement), value contrast, chroma, material (risograph ink, cut paper, flat silhouette, dry-print block). Never default to blue; name an exact hue (cobalt, ultramarine, pear green, tomato red, lemon yellow, magenta-pink, orange). Area ≈ 0.8–3% of poster or 10–30% of cluster; one main accent + ≤2 echoes (<25% of accent area); never pale/muted/pastel wording for it. Distributed Supporting Accent: only for a meaningful repeatable source motif (flowers, leaves, birds, lights); disperse with unequal scale/interval/density, one exact hue across the set, never an even border; area within the Standard allowance.

单色块模式 — only on the exact trigger `单色块模式`: three color categories only — natural paper; one unified neutral ink system (charcoal, graphite, warm gray, off-black) for all outlines, objects, texture, text; exactly one contiguous fully saturated color field at 3–12% of poster or 25–65% of cluster, solid and opaque — subject, aperture, window, sun, water, silhouette, or interior space; the visual entry, never a decorative swatch. No chromatic tints in supporting forms, no echoes, dots, stripes, extra colored objects. Typography: neutral ink and/or the one hue. Not monochrome-plus-accents.

## Typography

Fully free authorial material: no preset language, word count, font, alignment, hierarchy, placement, color, or legibility threshold. Any language, fragments, repeated words, long passages, or no text. Text may be caption, countervoice, title, interruption, rhythm, architectural form, texture, or the primary subject; tiny, oversized, cropped, scattered, rotated, obscured, fragmented, materially mixed. Only rule: deepen the proposition, tension, metaphor, or opening — not a neat caption.

## Hard avoids

Never reproduce, embed, crop, collage, trace, or retain photographic pixels or photorealistic regions from the reference — original illustration, paper, typography only. No exact composition copying, generic mood labels without embodiment, symbol clichés, random ambiguity, realistic shading, cartoon/kawaii/anime, arbitrary dots/grids, unsupported scattering, evenly repeated motifs, sticker outlines, halos, stamps, tape, multiple competing bright hues, scrapbooking, commercial hierarchy, logos, CTA, glossy mockups, curled paper, hard shadows, 3D, cinematic lighting, depth of field, neon, watermarks. In 单色块模式: no supporting tints, extra colored regions, accent echoes, detached swatches.
