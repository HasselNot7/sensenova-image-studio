Source-faithful cinematic photography poster: photograph, title, and small type as one composition. Effect built entirely through typography, hierarchy, negative space, alignment, selective depth — theatrical key art mood, never a real film. No named-film imitation, protected title lettering, decorative symbols, collage motifs, or stars; derive an original typographic identity from the scene.

## Plate

Portrait input → exact vertical 3:4 (unless asked), smallest safe crop from low-information sky/road/wall/periphery; preserve faces, landmark identity, gestures, perspective, axes, negative space; never stretch; non-portrait ratios stay unless asked. After cropping, the photo is the LOCKED SOURCE PHOTO: outside glyph masks change nothing — framing, geometry, people, vehicles, signage, clouds, buildings, windows, color, exposure, contrast, sharpness, noise, texture stay as shot. No regenerating, repainting, relighting, retouching, grading, blurring, sharpening, denoising, aging, or global paper effect.

Infer one short movie premise, for mood and wording only — never invent visual events or characters. Small text only on quiet, calm fields; faces, eyes, gestures, landmark crowns, readable signage stay unobscured; no letter counter or microtype across an eye or mouth.

## Archetype — pick one

Bottom-anchor / top-proclamation (title or byline in open sky or wall above subject) / central-emblem (title+hero one iconic center) / integrated-axis (title follows or counters a horizon, road, facade, beam) / edge-scale (title bleeds off-canvas, core readable). Never distribute text evenly around four edges. One dominant cluster, one supporting, one quiet zone.

## Copy deck — 3–5 roles, title required

Title: original, scene-derived, 1–4 words. Byline: alias, invented name, or non-personal phrase. Tagline: 3–10 words, ≤2 lines. Metadata: place, weather, time, coordinates, chapter, archive code. Microcopy: compact billing-block-like footer; scene nouns, fictional roles/names, invented imprint. Action marker: a date, chapter code, edition number, or similar concise anchor. Invented names/imprints welcome; never real people/studio/festival names, logos, awards, ratings, trademarks, or fake laurels.

## Title — a designed mark, not typed text

One genre voice: classical serif/Didone — memory, authority, romance; grotesk/slab — urban, crime, action; tracked geometric sans — science, distance; humanist serif/soft sans — intimacy, travel; hybrid — surreal. Max two type families: one title, one support (monospaced/ultra-condensed only in microcopy). Customize 1–3 details (cut, ligature, crossbar, counter, join) from visible structure: roofline, window rhythm, road, shadow. Instant recognition preserved; never distort every letter. No metallic, neon, bevel, glow, grunge, outlines, franchise styling.

Readable in 3s at thumbnail; two-line break semantic/rhythmic, no weak orphan; tight leading = monumental, open = quiet; title width aligns to a meaningful boundary (facade, horizon, credit block, deliberate bleed); off-canvas letters keep the word recoverable.

## Hierarchy (title = 100)

Byline/marker 20–32; tagline 12–20; metadata 8–12; microcopy 5–9. Adjacent levels visibly different; one L1 only; ≤1 L2 cluster; 3–5 levels as the image supports.

## Type–image

Connect type to hero via alignment, proximity, directional opposition — not centering in empty space. Support text aligns to real horizons, facades, window rhythm, perspective axes; title baselines optically level unless a diagonal is structurally justified. Selective depth: title strokes mostly one depth plane; hero edges pass in front where identity requires; title strokes return above calm areas; max 1–3 occlusions; small type always fully readable.

Contrast: title color sampled from photo or muted complement; reduce chroma before darkness. Legibility via value, placement, restrained opacity — never shadows or boxes. One quiet neutral for small type, ≤1 accent on the action marker; title survives grayscale and thumbnail.

## Material

Byline: uppercase/small caps, generous tracking, lighter than title. Tagline: quiet, 1–2 short lines, no effects. Microcopy: ultra-condensed legible face, tight leading, compact gray texture, clean edges; width aligned to title/lower boundary/central axis; away from busy high-contrast detail. Action marker: breathing room, above microcopy, below title.

Palette: 2–3 low-saturation colors — title ink, quiet neutral, ≤1 accent. Paper fibers/letterpress only inside large title glyphs; fine grain or clean ink for small; irregularity ends at glyph edges — never global texture. Layers: untouched photo → title+support type → hero edges above title → title strokes above calm areas → readable microcopy.

Bans: no boxes, panels, frames, circles, stickers, tape, laurels, awards, symbols, unrelated graphics, watermarks, pasted crops, outlines, glow, shadow, bevel; no unrequested real identities, logos, or ratings.
